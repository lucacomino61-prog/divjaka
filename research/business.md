# Business research: Hotel Pelikan and the Divjakë tourism economy

Researched 2026-10-23. Every line cites sources from the list at the end, written `[S#]`, all opened on 2026-10-23.
Confidence: **H** means two independent sources or an official one. **M** means one reputable source. **L** means unclear, conflicting, or weak.
Currency: Bank of Albania official rate for 22.10.2026 is **1 EUR = 90.64 ALL**, 1 USD = 80.73 ALL and 1 GBP = 108.19 ALL [S24] (H). Every conversion below uses this rate.

> Data-quality warnings
> - Booking.com served review snippets to the automated browser with Faker-style surnames ("Ondricka", "Hodkiewicz", "Kshlerin-morissette", "Aufderhar"). They also described things that do not fit the hotel: "view over the whole bay", a "freestanding tub", "open sea" [S2]. **Never quote Booking review text.** The aggregate scores agree with the other listings, so those are used.
> - The AlbaniaVisit lagoon page [S16] gives figures that conflict with official sources: 250–300 pelican pairs, a 12 m tower, park "established 1966". Its price ranges are used only at low (L) confidence.

2. "Over 20 years" in business: founding year? Was it under another company before?
3. Official phone, e-mail, WhatsApp/Viber numbers. Only +355 68 504 3955 (Google) was found.
4. Any Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or TripAdvisor pages? None were found.
5. Number of rooms in total; does the "Double Room" (1 full bed) exist in summer?
6. Is there a **pool** or a **fitness centre**? Google lists both; Booking and AlbaniaVisit list neither.
7. Playground? (Travelmyth AI summary only.)
8. Walking distance to the beach and to the Visitor Centre.
9. Summer (Jul–Aug) room prices and the opening season. Is the hotel open year-round?
10. Payment today is "cash only" on Booking. Which bank does the hotel use (BKT / Raiffeisen / other), and does it have POK or Paysera?
11. Which tours does the hotel actually run or resell, and with which partners (boat operator, guides, Albania Rafting Group)? Price and commission per tour.
12. Bike rental: does the hotel own bikes? Daily price? (Last public price: 200 ALL/h at the park, 2021.)
13. Current park / tower ticket price: 100, 300 or 50/150 ALL? Is a ticket charged at all?
14. Products for the shop: which local producers (dairy/cheese, buffalo, honey, olive oil, wine, fish) does the owner work with? None are publicly documented for Divjakë except watermelon.
15. Is eel or other lagoon fish offered as a takeaway or sale product? Check the licensing and the protected-species status before listing it.
