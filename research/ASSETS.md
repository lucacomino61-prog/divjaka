All photographs are real photos of the places named, taken from Wikimedia Commons under the licence shown. None is AI-generated. Each was resized and re-encoded to AVIF/WebP with metadata removed; nothing else was changed. Licence and author were read from each file's Commons page (2026-10-23).

## Notes for writers and the builder

- `pelican-xhimi`, `pelicans-visitor-centre`: these show the park's tame resident pelicans (Commons category "Pelican Xhimi"). Never caption them as wild birds on the lagoon. Only `pelicans-karavasta` shows pelicans out on the lagoon. Do not give the bird a name or say it "lives" there now: the category mixes "Xhimi" and "Xhoni", AP (2021) names the centre's pelicans Johnny and Vlashi, and nothing confirms who is there today.
- `ardenica-monastery` (near Lushnjë) and `apollonia` (near Fier) are not in Divjakë: captions must name their real place.
- `tower-360` is the 360-degree observation tower in the pine forest; `birdwatch-tower-boardwalk` is a wooden tower on the lagoon. Which one the client calls "Kulla e Vëzhgimit të Pelikanëve" is not settled by these sources.
- `lagoon-sunset-footbridge`: the source says Divjakë but not whether the water is the lagoon or the sea; the caption avoids both words.
- `egret-lagoon`: file title says heron, the Commons category says little egret (Egretta garzetta); the caption follows the category.
- `visitor-centre` (2018) and `visitor-centre-garden` (2019) may not match the building today.
- CC BY-SA files: the photos themselves stay under their licence; credit with a link to the licence on the credits page.

## Verification

Adversarial check, 2026-10-23. Result: **32 of 32 photos kept, none removed.** Every photo in `photos.json` now has `"verified": true`, `verified_on` and a `verification_note`.

What was checked:

- **Source pages:** all 32 Commons file pages were re-opened as HTML pages, not through the API. On every page the author, licence template, description and original-file URL match `photos.json`. No page has a deletion or problem template. Licences: CC BY-SA 4.0 (20), CC BY 4.0 (4), CC0 (4), CC BY 2.0 (2: Vollmer, Marmontel), CC BY-SA 2.0 (1: Seguinot), CC BY-SA 3.0 (1: Inkey). All of them allow commercial use. Each of the three Flickr copies has a passed FlickreviewR 2 review on its page.
- **Location:** 30 pages name Divjakë/Divjaka or Karavasta in the title, description or categories. `ardenica-monastery` and `apollonia` correctly stay `location_verified:false`. Their captions, "near Lushnjë" and "near Fier", are backed by `heritage.json` facts 108 and 111.
- **Files:** all 198 files in `public/photos` are listed in the JSON, and there are no orphans. Every file exists, is non-empty, and has the byte size and dimensions given in the JSON. Pillow 12.3 opens every file. None has EXIF data, a GPS block or XMP. `raw/` is git-ignored (`.gitignore` line 9 `raw/`), which settles the researcher's open question 5.
- **Pictures:** I looked at every 1280 WebP and at 100% crops of the six images with the least detail. None has a watermark or stamped credit. All are sharp enough for their role, but `flamingos-flight` is slightly soft at 100%, and `beach-from-tower` and `lagoon-view-from-above` are hazy.
