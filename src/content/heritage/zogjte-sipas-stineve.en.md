How many bird species have been recorded in the park? The bulletin of the National Agency of Protected Areas gives about 260;[^birds-15] other sources give anything from 228[^birds-15@1] to 265.[^birds-15@4] The park can hold up to 84,000 wintering waterbirds and about 12,000 breeding birds (AOS gives 12,000–15,000 pairs).[^birds-17][^birds-17@1] About 15 globally threatened species occur here, and at least 80 species listed in Annex I of the EU Birds Directive.[^birds-16]

::seasons

## Winter: a full lagoon

In the International Waterbird Census of January 2020, about 42,000 waterbirds of 55 species were counted at Divjakë-Karavasta, more than at any other wetland in Albania.[^birds-18] The greater spotted eagle (*Clanga clanga*), a globally threatened species, winters in the park; it has been seen every year since 2015.[^birds-23]

## Spring: migrants and colonies

The park is a stopover for birds migrating in spring from Africa to northern Europe. At the ringing camp of 17–21 March 2025, 42 birds of 14 species were ringed in the pine forest and the dune scrub; the most common was the Eurasian blackcap (*Sylvia atricapilla*).[^birds-26]

The park holds Albania’s largest colony of herons and cormorants, about 3,000 pairs: little egret, squacco heron, cattle egret, night heron, pygmy cormorant and glossy ibis. The colony sits in tamarisk scrub (*Tamarix*), the herons above and the cormorants nearer the water.[^birds-19]

## Summer: the lagoon islets

In 2020 the lagoon islets held 240–270 pairs of common tern, 60–70 pairs of yellow-legged gull, 45–55 pairs of little tern and 7–9 pairs of black-winged stilt.[^birds-20] The little tern had fallen from 300–400 pairs in the early 1990s to 60–90 pairs; a nesting platform built in April 2021 drew more than 100 pairs.[^birds-21]

## From the observation tower

In March 2020, greater flamingos, herons, pygmy cormorants, gulls, marsh harriers, ducks and waders were seen from the pelican observation tower (OpenStreetMap tags it as destroyed; its current state is not confirmed).[^birds-25] The Dalmatian pelican has [its own sheet](/en/heritage/pelikani-kacurrel/).

## Tips

For the pelicans, the best time is January to June; guides suggest early morning.[^prac-13] Some trails may be closed during the breeding season, and the closure is not always signed at the start of the trail.[^park-24] Hunting is forbidden in every protected area.[^park-20]
