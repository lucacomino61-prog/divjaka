Divjakë town lies about 85 km by road from Tirana[^prac-01] and about 5 km from the Adriatic Sea.[^hist-02] The distances below are by road from the centre of Divjakë town, with times without traffic; real journeys take longer.

::distances

## From the town to the park and the hotel

The beach road starts in the centre of Divjakë, crosses the canal, passes the Visitor Centre and ends at the beach, where Hotel Pelikan stands. From the town to the Visitor Centre is about 4 km, about 10 minutes by car.[^prac-06] This is the only road to the park and to Divjaka beach; the Ministry of Environment has put a barrier with electronic ticketing on it.[^coast-04] The ticket was announced at 50 lekë for tourists; we have not verified whether it is collected today.[^park-19]

::chart divjake-centre visitor-centre hotel-pelikan

## By bus

A booking site lists about 10 departures a day from Tirana to Divjakë, the first at 06:00, taking about 1 h 30 min; Divjakë has a bus station. We found no confirmed public transport from the town to the park.[^prac-08]

## When to come

The climate is Mediterranean: average temperatures run from about 12 °C in February to about 24 °C in August.[^prac-09] For the pelicans, the best time is January to June, early in the morning.[^prac-13] In summer, mosquitoes are active near the lagoon[^prac-11], and it is the season of the highest forest-fire risk.[^prac-10]

For every month, see [Birds through the year](/en/heritage/zogjte-sipas-stineve/).
